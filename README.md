# Seven Wonders Silhouette Identification Quiz

Shows a black SVG silhouette drawing of one of 14 world wonders (mix of ancient and modern) and presents four answer choices. After guessing, a fact card reveals the wonder's location, approximate year built, and an interesting historical fact. The game runs for 14 rounds with a final score and rating.

## Features

- 14 unique SVG silhouettes drawn in-browser for wonders including the Pyramids, Eiffel Tower, Taj Mahal, Angkor Wat, Petra, and more
- Four-choice multiple choice with correct answer highlighted green and wrong guess highlighted red
- Fact card revealed after each answer: location, year, and historical trivia
- Progress bar and running score throughout the 14-round session
- Final score with a rating from "Keep exploring" to "World Wonder Expert"
